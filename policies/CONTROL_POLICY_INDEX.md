## 1. Overview & Policy Governance

Pingplus Media maintains an active Information Security Management System (ISMS) certified to ISO/IEC 27001:2022 (Certificate `305026090945IS`, QRO Certification LLP, valid through 8 September 2029). All compliance and security policies referenced below are approved by the Office of the Founder & CEO, published publicly on the official company domain (`https://pingplusmedia.com/`), and actively communicated across operations.

These published policy URLs provide external verification of documented governance standards. Operational implementation details, sanitized technical test transcripts, and audit logs are documented in the respective evidence folders of this repository.
