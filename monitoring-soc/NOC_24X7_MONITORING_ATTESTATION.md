## 2. Operational Reality & Owner Attestation

The system owner officially confirms the following operational staffing and infrastructure monitoring model:
- **In-House Operations:** Continuous 24 hours per day, 365 days per year operations (24x7x365).
- **Shift Schedule:** Three rotating eight-hour operational shifts:
  - **Shift 1 (Morning):** 06:00 – 14:00 IST (Office-based monitoring)
  - **Shift 2 (Evening):** 14:00 – 22:00 IST (Office-based monitoring)
  - **Shift 3 (Night):** 22:00 – 06:00 IST (Authorized secure remote monitoring & on-call)
- **Relief Coverage:** Dedicated relief staffing roster active for statutory holidays, sick leave, and planned absences.
- **Monitoring Scope:** Real-time monitoring of SMPP bind states, message throughput (TPS), latency, carrier delivery receipt (DLR) completion, system load, and Wazuh SIEM security events.
- **Monthly Log Reviews:** Security log reviews conducted monthly by senior operations management (last recorded session: **9 October 2026**).
